### 例句跟读

1. **above**：The plane flew **above** the clouds.（飞机在云层上方飞行。）
2. **accident**：He had a car **accident** on his way to school.（他在上学路上出了车祸。）
3. **account**：Please send me my bank **account** statement.（请把银行账目单寄给我。）
4. **achieve**：If you work hard, you will **achieve** your goal.（努力就能实现目标。）
5. **actor**：The **actor** played the lead role very well.（这位男演员把主角演得很好。）
6. **actually**：**Actually**, I finished the homework yesterday.（实际上，我昨天就把作业做完了。）
7. **address**：What's your new home **address**?（你新家的地址是什么？）
8. **admire**：I really **admire** her for her patience.（我真的很钦佩她的耐心。）
9. **advantage**：Living near school is a big **advantage**.（住在学校附近是个很大的优势。）
10. **afraid**：She is **afraid** of speaking in public.（她害怕当众讲话。）
11. **against**：We are **against** the plan because it costs too much.（我们反对这个计划，因为它花费太高。）
12. **along**：We walked **along** the beach and talked.（我们沿着海滩边走边聊。）

| ---- | ---- | ---- | -------- | ------------------ |
| aloud | /əˈlaʊd/ | adv. | 大声地；出声地 | read aloud（大声朗读）；aloud 放在动词后，不用 very aloud |
| although | /ɔːlˈðəʊ/ | conj. | 虽然；尽管 | 不能和 but 同时出现（有 although 无 but） |

| artist | /ˈɑːtɪst/ | n. | 艺术家；（尤指）画家 | art + ist（表示人）；同源：painter |
| asleep | /əˈsliːp/ | adj. | 睡着的 | fall asleep（入睡）；区别 sleepy（困倦的，还没睡着） |

| award | /əˈwɔːd/ | n./v. | 奖；奖品；授予 | 区别 reward（报酬）：award 颁奖，reward 回报 |
| aware | /əˈweə(r)/ | adj. | 意识到的 | be aware of（意识到…）；名词 awareness |

### 例句跟读

1. **aloud**：Please read the text **aloud** in class.（请在课上朗读课文。）
2. **although**：**Although** it was raining, we still went running.（虽然下着雨，我们还是去跑步了。）
3. **among**：He is the tallest **among** the three boys.（三个男孩里他最高。）
4. **ancient**：Beijing is an **ancient** city with a long history.（北京是一座历史悠久的古城。）
5. **anyway**：It's too late to go out; **anyway**, it's raining.（出去太晚了，再说了，还在下雨。）
6. **anywhere**：I can't find my keys **anywhere**.（我哪儿都找不到钥匙。）
7. **apartment**：They live in a small **apartment** near the school.（他们住在学校附近一套小公寓里。）
8. **appear**：Stars **appear** in the sky after dark.（天黑后星星出现在天上。）
9. **argue**：Don't **argue** with your parents about this.（别为此和父母争吵。）
10. **article**：She wrote an **article** about saving water.（她写了一篇关于节水的文章。）
11. **artist**：The **artist** drew pictures for children's books.（这位画家为儿童书画插图。）
12. **asleep**：The baby fell **asleep** during the movie.（电影放映期间宝宝睡着了。）
13. **athlete**：Every **athlete** trained hard for the match.（每位运动员都为比赛刻苦训练。）
14. **attack**：A dog **attacked** the postman yesterday.（昨天一只狗袭击了邮递员。）
15. **attend**：All students must **attend** the flag-raising ceremony.（全体学生必须参加升旗仪式。）
16. **attention**：Please pay **attention** to the teacher's instructions.（请注意听老师的指示。）
17. **autumn**：The leaves turn gold in **autumn**.（秋天叶子变成金色。）
18. **average**：His score is above the class **average**.（他的成绩高于班级平均分。）
19. **avoid**：We should **avoid** talking about his mistakes in public.（我们应该避免当众谈论他的错误。）
20. **award**：He won the first prize **award** in the singing contest.（他在歌唱比赛中获得一等奖。）
21. **aware**：Are you **aware** of the danger of driving fast?（你意识到超速行车的危险了吗？）
