### 语法题

**宾语从句的语序**

> Could you tell me ___ as volunteers tomorrow?
> Yes. We're going to clean up litter in the park.
>
> A. what we did  B. what we do  C. what we will do  D. what will we do
>
> **答案：C**

**考点**：宾语从句用**陈述语序**（主语 + 谓语），不能用疑问语序。根据 tomorrow 可知用一般将来时 will do。
